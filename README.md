## Reactive UI
So this week, each team member has to brainstorm a few ideas for the reactive phone UI. I mainly had 3 ideas. The first is involves having the user click and drag across the screen to create a trail of particles. The colors of the particle will become more vibrant when sound starts playing. the second idea was to have a ripple effect that starts small at the starts of the phones playing and reaches a max point during the middle of the audio experience, where the ripples are large and numerous. The ripple calms down the close the audio reaches the end. The last idea is similar to the second one but with particle effects.
